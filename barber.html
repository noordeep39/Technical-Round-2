About Kabir

Sat Sri Akal, everyone. I’m Kabir Mehra, born and raised in Chandigarh. At 19, I began sweeping floors at a neighbourhood barbershop. Bashir Chacha, a veteran ustad, taught me to learn the craft properly.

At 26, I moved to Dubai. Over twelve years I worked from a small Bur Dubai shop to head barber at a premium Jumeirah grooming lounge. Businessmen, footballers, tourists and film crews sat in my chair.

I learned that a great haircut is only half the job. The other half is helping someone walk out standing a little taller. When my daughter was born, I knew it was time to come home. This winter, I’m bringing Dubai-standard grooming to Chandigarh at honest prices.

We use hot towels, offer proper consultations, and sterilise our tools after every customer. Every shave gets a fresh towel and a new blade.

Kabir Mehra, Founder and Head Barber
